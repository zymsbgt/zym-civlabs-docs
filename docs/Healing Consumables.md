# Healing Consumables

## Note: These features apply exclusively to Zym's CivLabs and are absent in other versions of CivLabs.

## ❤️ Foods that replenish hearts

Cookie: 5 in 8 cookies heal you 0.5 hearts

Bread, Baked Potato: 1 heart

Beetroot/Mushroom Soup, Golden Carrot, Bread, Suspicious Stew: 1.5 hearts

Pumpkin Pie: 2.5 - 3.0 Hearts

Rabbit Stew: 4.0 Hearts

## 🎂 Cakes

1.5 hearts and restores hunger normally, or 1 heart while full. Yes, you can eat cake even if your hunger bar is full.

## ✨ The Saturation Trade-off

If you max out your saturation, you will get +1 Heart and your saturation will reset to zero.

Look out for the message: “Saturation converted to +1 Heart!” accompanied by a sparkle sound.

## 💧 Water Bottles

You can now drink a Water Bottle (the plain kind, no effects) to heal.

Cost: 2 Hunger Shanks.

Reward: 0.5 Hearts.

Restriction: You cannot do this if you have fewer than 2.5 hunger shanks left - you can't heal if you're starving!
